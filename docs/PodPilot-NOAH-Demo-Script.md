# PodPilot AI — three-minute hackathon demonstration

**Tagline:** Everyone has a story. Now everyone has a host.

**Presenters:** Founder and Sumedha (creative director). Switch roles if preferred. This script demonstrates the no-API-cost three-question mode honestly. A live contextual AI interview is an optional substitution after provider configuration and testing.

## Rehearsal setup

- Open Host Settings and the standalone studio. Use the approved NOAH portrait. Keep the HeyGen sample labeled prerecorded; it is not proof of live AI.
- On the presentation laptop, enable camera/microphone, wear headphones and record a soundcheck. Replay the download and confirm both voices.
- Keep a genuine previous episode and rendered clip as backup, labeled **earlier recording**. Do not say these were generated during the live demo.
- Default answer windows are 30–45 seconds. Use **Done — next question** after each short answer to fit this demonstration.
- A 15-second clip renders in roughly real time. Prepare a genuine clip beforehand or allow rendering while explaining the workflow.
- If hardware fails, use the labeled host-only preview or text interview and state the limitation.

**Founder:** “Founders can explain why their product exists. Creators can record opinions and stories. Experts and coaches can share examples. Podcasters can make companion clips. **Sumedha:** “Today the browser exports real video clips and WAV audio. Publishing is a separate step. MP3 conversion, more characters, more backgrounds and real-time avatar video are future extensions.”

## Answers for judges

- **Does it automatically clone NOAH?** No. Voice samples are references; cloning needs provider support, authorization and setup.
- **Is the portrait lip-synced video?** No. It is a stylized animated still with an actual audio indicator. The HeyGen video is a labeled prerecorded sample/intro.
- **Does every demo record the host?** The three-question demo mixes bundled audio directly. Long-form browser speech requires explicit tab-audio capture; otherwise the recording is labeled guest-only. Configured provider audio is routed into the recording bus.
- **Does ChatGPT Pro pay for the APIs?** No. Provider usage and billing are separate.
- **Where are the uploads and recordings?** In this browser for the MVP. Download the files; cross-device authenticated storage is future work.
- **Does it publish automatically or guarantee viral content?** No. It exports files and editable drafts for creators to review and publish.
